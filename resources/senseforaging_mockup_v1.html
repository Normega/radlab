The Book

From why to how.

Better in Every Sense made the case: when we get stuck in the House of Habit, sensation is the way out. The Handbook is the field guide — a structured program of sense-foraging practices that trains the shift from analytic scanning to receptive attention, one sense at a time. Not affirmations, not willpower: small, repeatable perceptual skills backed by the neuroscience of interoception and attention.

1

Learn

Each chapter opens with the science — what your brain is doing when it predicts instead of perceives, and why that traps you.

2

Practice

Each chapter pairs with an interactive practice in the web companion. No account, no score, no streak — the mechanic itself teaches the skill.

3

Carry

Each practice ends off-screen: a prompt for foraging in the world — a walk, a meal, a room — with the skill still warm.

These are live, playable practices from the companion — built and studied at RADlab, University of Toronto. They are deliberately non-striving: attention itself is the only control. Rest, don't search.
