Breath change stream

A circle breathes in and out. Now and then its pace changes. Press as soon as you notice a change. That is the whole task.

This page is a prototype of the BCAT-DDM task: one continuous pacing stream, built for modelling how evidence that the breath has changed builds up into noticing. The options below are the designs from the simulations.

What to run
Pre-run length

The pre-run measures the person's thresholds: a shorter stream of sudden changes whose sizes adapt to the responses so far, separately for speeding and slowing. The stream after it is scaled to the two thresholds it finds. The simulations favour 24 changes; 12 is for a quick look.
