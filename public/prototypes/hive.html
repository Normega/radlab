Hive

Keep this open beside whatever you’re doing.

When you notice you’ve been doing for a while, tap Out. The hive offers one sense. Put the phone down and stay with it for as long as you like.

Then tap Home, bring back a word if one comes, and go back to what you were doing. Each trip becomes a cell in the comb.

There are no reminders: every trip starts with you noticing. Nothing leaves this phone.
