Come, See — sketchbook

Six small experiences, each three to five minutes. Sound on, if you can. Nothing can be done wrong.

receiving impressions
Thaw

Evening. The glass has fogged over, and the garden is still out there. Slow hands see more.
letting go
Milkweed

The pods are ripe. The wind is patient. What you keep dims; what you release flies.
call and quiet response
Reply

The valley is listening. Offer a small sound, softly, and then be quiet long enough to hear.
follow without catching
Alongside

Something is moving through the meadow. It cannot be caught. It can be kept company.
averted vision
Sidelong

The moon is out, and the sky is fuller than it looks. What is faint goes out when you point at it.
hosting
Stone

You are a stone in a stream. The afternoon is long, and things will come to use you.
