Come, See — sketchbook

Eight small experiences, each three to five minutes. Sound on, if you can. Nothing can be done wrong.

receiving impressions
Thaw

Evening. The glass has fogged over, and the garden is still out there. Rub the glass slowly, and see what is there.
letting go
Milkweed

The pods are ripe. The wind is patient. What you keep dims; what you release flies.
call and quiet response
Reply

The valley is listening. Offer a small sound, then listen.
follow without catching
Alongside

Something is moving through the meadow. It cannot be caught. It can be kept company.
averted vision
Sidelong

The moon is out, and the sky is fuller than it looks. What is faint goes out when you point at it.
hosting
Stone

You are a stone in a stream. The afternoon is long, and things will come and rest on you.
seeing at low frequency
Soften

Evening in the valley, and the wind keeps bringing clouds over. Half-close your eyes and let the shapes run together.
motion in the corner of the eye
Wallflower

Something is dancing out there, and it stops when you look. Keep near it, and keep your eyes elsewhere.
noticing the mode you are in
Toggle

Two ways of being here: the story and the senses. Tap when you notice you have switched.

RADlab prototype testbeds for the Come, See sense-foraging platform — the receptive dynamics from the design compendium (§4), each breaking one link of see → want → act → get, plus two from the soft-gaze family (2026-08-15): defocus, and peripheral motion. Feedback lives in the world; nothing is scored, counted, or displayed. Append ?dev for the tuning panel (or press t). All copy is placeholder.
