Our class trial

A randomised controlled trial, run by this class, on this class

Four weeks, about five minutes a day. Have the device you'll use each day in your hand.

What this is. A small randomised controlled trial of three daily practices for stress, run by our class, so you experience an RCT from the inside before we analyse one together on Nov 18. It's a learning exercise, not research: your answers are only shown to the class in aggregate, never published, and deleted by Jan 31, 2027.

The site opens at 10:25; the Quercus announcement with the same link posts at 10:55.

Start here

Join now

  1. Scan the code, or go to radlab.zone/join/classtrial. Press Start and enter your U of T student email (@mail.utoronto.ca).
  2. Open the email from research@radlab.zone. It has your personal link. Not there in two minutes? Check junk or quarantine, then put your hand up.
  3. Read the consent form. Taking part is your choice, and so is stopping, at any time.
  4. Do the baseline survey now: 10 to 15 minutes. Everything else waits on it.

radlab.zone/join/classtrial

While they work, walk the room. The email that arrives is the check that their inbox receives radlab's messages. Anyone with no email within a couple of minutes: look at junk or quarantine together, and mark it "not spam". Anyone with an error: note their email and the message (psy240@radlab.zone).

Personal questions. Some baseline questions ask about mood and anxiety. "Prefer not to answer" is on every question, the face ratings included, nobody reads answers as they arrive, and everyone sees the same support list at the end.

What happens next

The next five weeks

  • Today to Fri Oct 16: the baseline. You'll be in one of three groups, chosen at random when you finish it.
  • Sat Oct 17 to Fri Nov 13: one email at 7 am each day. About five minutes: two quick ratings, a short practice, the same two ratings. The link works until 6 am the next morning, with one reminder at 7 pm.
  • Missed a day? It counts as missed, and the next one arrives as usual. There's nothing to catch up.
  • Sat Nov 14 to Mon Nov 16: the final survey (about 10 minutes), then the debrief.
  • Wed Nov 18: we look at the class's results together.

Why we don't tell you the groups. Knowing which practice is which, or which one I expect to help, would change the results. You'll find out at the debrief, and it's one of the things we'll talk about.

Late joiners can still join after Friday: they start on that day of the calendar, and the days before count as missed.

Consent

Your choice, all the way through

  • Taking part earns the trial's participation credit.
  • Not taking part, or stopping at any point, moves the credit to a written alternative on Quercus: a 2.5 to 3 page proposal, due Mon Nov 16. No reason needed.
  • To stop, use the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Your answers are shown to the class only in aggregate, never published, and deleted by Jan 31, 2027.

Stuck? Put your hand up, or write to psy240@radlab.zone.

Before they leave: "Your first practice arrives Saturday at 7 am. Check that today's email came from research@radlab.zone and isn't in spam."

Everyone writes the RCT reflection from the class's results on Nov 18, whether or not they took part.
