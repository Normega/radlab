Substance use and academic outcomes

The proposal

800 students, recruited with a $500 prize draw, complete an online survey covering alcohol use, cannabis use, unprescribed stimulant use, use before exams, academic discipline history, and mental health history.

Responses are stored with the student number, to allow later linking to academic transcripts. The data live on a research assistant’s laptop and in a shared Drive folder, and are kept indefinitely “for future studies.” The consent form describes the data as “confidential.”

Risk statement, as submitted

“None; this is only a survey.”

Your job — 20 minutes

  1. Read the protocol closely.
  2. Verdict: approve / approve with changes / reject.
  3. Name the TCPS2 principle most at stake — Respect for Persons, Concern for Welfare, or Justice — and the specific feature of this protocol that puts it there.
  4. If approving with changes: changes concrete enough that the researcher could act on them tomorrow.
