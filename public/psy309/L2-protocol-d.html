Secondary analysis of community health data

The proposal

No new data collection. The researchers analyse an existing, de-identified provincial health dataset covering three First Nations communities, examining rates of diabetes, depression, and substance use. Community-level comparisons will be published with the communities named.

The proposal includes no community consultation.

Rationale, as submitted

“The data are de-identified and already collected — there is no risk, and no individual consent is required.”

Your job — 20 minutes

  1. Read the protocol closely.
  2. Verdict: approve / approve with changes / reject.
  3. Name the TCPS2 principle most at stake — Respect for Persons, Concern for Welfare, or Justice — and the specific feature of this protocol that puts it there.
  4. If approving with changes: changes concrete enough that the researcher could act on them tomorrow.
