## Debrief form

### About This Study

**Approved (Study 1):**

> This study is testing a new questionnaire designed to measure people's ability to notice and shift into a receptive, sensory way of engaging with the world (sometimes called "sense foraging"), as distinct from a more goal-directed, evaluative way of engaging with it. In this first study we are examining how the questionnaire's 48 statements group together (its factor structure) and which statements best capture each part, so that we can produce a shorter version. Some statements were worded in the opposite direction from others (for example, describing difficulty rather than ease): mixing directions helps us separate what people agree with from a general tendency to agree. A later study will examine how the questionnaire relates to established measures of mindfulness and bodily awareness.

**Study 2a:**

> This study is testing a new questionnaire designed to measure people's ability to notice and shift into a receptive, sensory way of engaging with the world (sometimes called "sense foraging"), as distinct from a more goal-directed, evaluative way of engaging with it. In an earlier study its statements formed four parts: getting into the sensing mode, understanding the two modes, valuing and savouring time spent sensing, and letting experience be. This study tests revised and new statements for each part, and for a possible fifth part, foraging (looking for something to take in), so that we can choose the best statements for the final version. Your ratings of the brief descriptions of people tell us whether the difference between the two modes was clear, and the uncertainty questionnaire (IUS-12) helps us tell letting experience be apart from a general dislike of uncertainty. Some statements were worded in the opposite direction from others (for example, describing difficulty rather than ease): mixing directions helps us separate what people agree with from a general tendency to agree. A later study will examine how the questionnaire relates to established measures of mindfulness and bodily awareness.
