Follow-up: Development and Validation of the Sense Foraging Scale

UNIVERSITY OF TORONTO, Department of Psychology. Principal Investigator: Prof. Norman Farb, PhD, Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences, University of Toronto Mississauga, norman.farb@utoronto.ca, 905-828-3959.

Why you are invited

About two weeks ago you took part in our study of a new questionnaire about how people pay attention. You were chosen at random to be invited to this optional follow-up. Answering the same statements again a short time later tells us how stable the questionnaire’s scores are, which is how researchers judge whether a change in scores is real. Three short questions about your past week tell us how the scores relate to everyday life.

What you will be asked to do

Answer the same 20 statements about how you pay attention, then the same three short questions about your past week as before (for example, on how many days you paused to take in your surroundings). It takes about 4 minutes, in a single session.

Voluntary participation and risks

Taking part is entirely voluntary, and declining has no effect on your earlier participation or payment. Every statement offers a "Prefer not to answer" option, and you may stop at any time by closing the browser window. The statements and questions are the same everyday-experience ones as before; we do not expect any risk beyond everyday life. A list of mental health resources is shown at the end.

Confidentiality

We do not collect your name or email address. Your Prolific ID is used only to process your payment and to link your follow-up answers to your earlier ones by a random code that contains no information about you; the Prolific ID is then permanently deleted, as described in the original consent form. All data are stored in encrypted form on RADlab, the lab’s own research platform, accessible only to the research team, and results are reported only in aggregate. The choice you made earlier about depositing your de-identified data in the University of Toronto’s Dataverse applies to your follow-up answers too.

Compensation

You will be paid at the same rate as before, $12.00 USD per hour, prorated (approximately $0.80 for an estimated 4-minute follow-up), through Prolific.

Withdrawing your data

To withdraw your follow-up answers, email norman.farb@utoronto.ca with your Prolific ID within 48 hours of completing the follow-up. After de-identification your responses can no longer be located or removed.
